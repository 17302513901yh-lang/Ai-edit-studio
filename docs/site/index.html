A few things to know.

Node.js 20+ and FFmpeg are required. Windows beta; CI also runs on macOS and Linux.

Local transcription needs the optional whisper.cpp setup. No face tracking or generative video. Optional online music generation sends your prompt only when you choose a provider.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
you press play.

Does my footage leave my computer?+

The editing and rendering engine runs locally and reads your originals without modifying them. Connecting a cloud AI agent is your choice; what that agent sends to its provider follows its own configuration. Optional online music generation requires your explicit provider selection.

Can I use it without an AI agent?+

Yes. The browser editor has a real timeline, keyboard shortcuts, captions, colour controls and export tools. An agent is an optional collaborator, not a requirement.

What can I export?+

SDR H.264 video in widescreen, vertical, square and portrait formats, with output options up to 4K. 4K is Lanczos upscaling, not AI detail. The current editing limit is 20 minutes, 200 shots and 24 sources.

Is it really free?+

AI Edit Studio is free and MIT licensed. You can inspect the source and contribute. Optional third-party AI agents or online music providers may have their own costs.

Where do I report a problem?+

It is beta software and outside testing helps. Read the troubleshooting guide or open a GitHub issue with your system, steps and error message.
